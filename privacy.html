Flip It Out

Privacy

Last updated October 2026.

Your documents

Files you check are read and turned into reports on your own device. The document itself is never uploaded to us.

Searches

To find matching sources, short phrases from the document are sent as search queries to the databases turned on in Settings: Wikipedia, OpenAlex, Crossref, Europe PMC, Semantic Scholar and, if you add your own key, Google Programmable Search. Each service handles those queries under its own privacy policy.

Stored on your device

Earlier submissions, their reports, your settings and your plan are kept in your browser so later scans can be compared with them. Clear them at any time under Submissions, Delete all, or by clearing the site data in your browser.

Payments and licenses

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller of record and processes your payment details; we never see your card. When you activate Pro, your license key is sent to our license check and to Lemon Squeezy to confirm it is valid.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/pogi6969/FLIP-IT-OUT.
